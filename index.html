A Letter For You 💌

From my heart to yours

Ennoda azhagu sellath thangam Anjali, 🤍

Ennoda life-la naan idhuvaraikkum ethirpaarththa niraiya vishayangal nadandhadhe kidaiyaadhu. But nee en life-kulla vandhadhukku appuram, naan ethirpaarththa pala santhoshangal enakku kidaichirukku. Ippo varaikkum naan happy-ah irukken. ❤️

Naan unnai happy-ah vachirukkena, unakku pidicha maadhiri unnai paathukkirena-nu enakku theriyala. But nee ennai evvalavu happy-ah vachirukka-nu enakku nallaave theriyum. Nee eppovume ennai comfortable-ah feel panna vachirukka. Naan eppadi irundhaalum, en situation eppadi irundhaalum, ennai appadiye accept panni anbu koduthirukka.

Unakkaaga niraiya pannanum, unnai nalla paathukkanum, un aasai ellathaiyum niraiveththanum-nu enakku niraiya aasai irukku. Aana adhai eppadi words-la express panradhu-nu mattum enakku sathiyama theriyala.

Naan unnai ivvalavu nesikkiradhukku enna kaaranam-nu enakke theriyala. Aana unnai vittup piriyakoodaadhu-ngira oru confidence en manasukkulla eppovum odikitte irukku. Nee enakku avvalavu mukkiyamaanava, Anjali. 🤍

Nee veetla problems face pannumbodhum, unakkaaga yaaravadhu edhaavadhu sonnaalum, enna nadandhaalum unakkaaga naan iruppen-nu en manasukkulla mudivu pannitten. Inime enna nadandhaalum sari, naan un kooda kadaisi varaikkum irukkanum. Nee edhukkaagavum bayappada vendaam. Unakku pidicha vishayangalai sei; un kanavugalai thairiyama follow pannu. Un pakkathula unakkaaga irukkura oruthana naan irukkanum-nu aasapaduren.

Enakkaaga nee evvalavu valigalai thaangiyirukka, evvalavu vishayangalai samaalichirukka-nu ippo enakku puriyudhu. Sila vishayangalai ninaichale en kannula thanni varudhu. Indha message-ai unakku ezhudhittu irukkumbodhu, unnai nerla paathu irukkama kattippidichu, en manasula irukkira ellathaiyum sollanum-nu thonudhu. 🥹❤️

Nee en life-la oru mukkiyamaana part mattum kidaiyaadhu. En santhoshathoda oru periya kaaranam nee. Nee eppovum sirichukitte, santhoshama, nimmadhiya irukkanum. Adhudhaan en life-la naan adaiya virumbura periya achievement.

Namma life-la enna nadandhaalum serndhu paathukkalaam. Unakku enna pidikkudho adhai sei. Un santhoshamum nimmadhiyum enakku romba mukkiyam. Namma love unakku santhoshathai kodukkanum; bayathaiyo pressure-aiyo kodukkakoodaadhu. Nee en kooda irukkumbodhu un manasukku nimmadhi irukkanum.

Idhuvaraikkum en life-la yaarukkum kodukkaadha oru mukkiyamaana idathai unakku koduthirukken. Inimeyum unnai anboda paathukkanum, unakkaaga niraiya nalla vishayangal pannanum, un kooda serndhu niraiya azhagaana memories create pannanum-nu aasai paduren.

Innum unkitta pesa niraiya irukku. Solla niraiya irukku. Aana ennaala words-la ellathaiyum solla mudiyala. En manasula irukkira anbai muzhuvadhum velippaduthura alavukku vaarthaigal podhala.

Innaikku namma love-oda unnudaiya first birthday. Ennaala un kooda nerla irundhu indha naalai celebrate panna mudiyalainaalum, indha chinna surprise un mugathula oru smile-ai kondu varanum-nu aasai paduren. 🎂🤍

Wish you many more happy returns of the day, ennoda azhagu sellath thangam Anjali! ❤️
